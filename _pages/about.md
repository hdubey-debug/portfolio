I'm Harsh Dubey, a Firmware Engineer with expertise in wireless networks and IoT solutions. I graduated from South Dakota State University with a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering in May 2021.

My professional experience spans across leading technology companies including Trilliant Networks and Amazon, where I've developed firmware for wireless networks, created IoT solutions, and worked on cutting-edge technologies like 802.15.4 networks, LoRaWAN, and embedded systems.

I'm passionate about developing innovative solutions for embedded systems, wireless communication protocols, and IoT applications. My work involves everything from low-level firmware development to creating comprehensive IoT dashboards and remote control systems.
